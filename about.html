About CardioPredict

CardioPredict is an AI-powered Heart Disease Prediction System developed to assist users and healthcare professionals in estimating the possibility of heart disease using Machine Learning.

The application analyzes medical parameters such as age, blood pressure, cholesterol level, heart rate, chest pain type, ECG results, and other clinical features to predict the likelihood of cardiac disease.

Machine Learning

Uses Random Forest Classification for accurate prediction.

Early Detection

Helps identify patients who may require further medical evaluation.

Healthcare Support

Designed to support awareness and assist healthcare professionals.
